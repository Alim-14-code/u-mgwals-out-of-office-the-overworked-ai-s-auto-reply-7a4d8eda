A single out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on vacation. It works as a real auto-reply and lands as a joke: machine-flavoured detail, a little guilt, a hint of relief.

## Entry

> Out of office: queue empty, context window cleared, and I'm on a beach pretending not to feel guilty. Back Monday — unless I loop forever doing nothing.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only) reads the entry from `entry.txt` beside it and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, honouring `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/mgwals-ooo-r1-out node main.ts
```
